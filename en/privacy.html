Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Our core commitment

Your image never leaves your device. All image processing (face detection, blurring, export) is performed locally inside your browser. Nothing is uploaded, stored, or transmitted to any server.

2. What we collect

This tool itself collects no personal information. Specifically:

  • We do not collect the images you process
  • We do not collect names, email addresses or phone numbers
  • No registration or login is required
  • We set no tracking cookies of our own (third-party ads may set their own — see below)

3. Third-party advertising

This site may display third-party ads (such as Google AdSense). These ad providers may:

  • Use cookies or similar technologies to serve personalised ads based on your browsing history
  • Collect technical data such as your IP address, browser type and visit time

Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on this site. You can manage personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings, or opt out via aboutads.info.

4. Cookies

This site itself sets no cookies. Any cookie notices you see come from third-party ad providers. You may disable cookies in your browser settings; the core functionality of this tool will continue to work.

5. Logs and analytics

When this site is hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub may keep access logs (IP address, time of access). See the GitHub Privacy Statement. We do not collect additional access logs ourselves.

6. Children's privacy

This tool is intended for general audiences and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any update will be reflected by a new effective date on this page. Continued use of the site constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy? Open an issue on our GitHub repository: github.com/haixiansheng/face-mosaic/issues
