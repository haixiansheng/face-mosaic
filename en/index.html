📖 How to use

What does this tool do?

Upload a video and the tool automatically detects faces in every frame and blurs them out. When processing finishes you can download the result. It is useful for hiding bystanders before posting street videos, protecting children's privacy in family clips, or censoring customer faces in surveillance footage.

Steps

  1. Upload a video — click the upload area, or drag & drop your file.
  2. Tune the settings — mosaic strength (higher = blurrier), detection sensitivity (lower it if faces are missed), blur area padding (increase if faces are partially covered), and the effect type (pixelation / blur / black box).
  3. Start processing — click “Start processing” and wait for the progress bar. Processing takes roughly as long as the video duration.
  4. Download — click “Download video” to save the result.

FAQ

Q: Is my video uploaded to a server?
A: No. Everything happens inside your browser. Your video never leaves your device, and the tool even works offline.

Q: Why does processing take as long as the video itself?
A: To guarantee that no frame is skipped, the tool processes in real time (playback → detection → blur → recording). A 1-minute video takes about 1 minute.

Q: Some faces were not blurred. What should I do?
A: Increase “Blur area padding” to 20–30%, or lower “Detection sensitivity” to 0.3, then process again. Profiles, heavy occlusion and very small faces are harder to detect.

Q: What output format do I get?
A: WebM (VP8/VP9) by default, which plays on all major platforms. MP4 may be produced when your browser supports it.

Q: What if I switch tabs while processing?
A: Browsers throttle background tabs, which can drop frames in the recording. Please keep the tab in the foreground while processing.

Supported browsers

Recent versions of Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari (MediaRecorder + canvas capture). Chrome or Edge give the best results.
