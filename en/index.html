📖 How to use

What does this tool do?

Upload a photo and the tool automatically detects faces and blurs them out — instantly. Download the result right away. Useful for hiding bystanders before posting photos, censoring ID photos or group shots, and removing private details from screenshots.

Steps

  1. Upload an image — click the upload area, drag & drop, or paste with Ctrl+V.
  2. Auto blur — the tool detects faces and blurs them as soon as the image loads.
  3. Tune it — drag the sliders for a live preview: mosaic strength, detection sensitivity (lower it if faces are missed), blur padding, and effect type.
  4. Download — click “Download image” to save the result at full resolution.

FAQ

Q: Is my image uploaded to a server?
A: No. Everything happens inside your browser. Your image never leaves your device, and the tool works offline.

Q: Why is this so much faster than video tools?
A: A photo is a single frame, whereas video requires per-frame processing and real-time encoding. That's why this tool returns a result in milliseconds.

Q: Some faces were not blurred. What should I do?
A: Try, in order: ① make sure “Detection mode” is set to Accurate (the default, SSD model); ② lower “Detection sensitivity” to 0.3; ③ increase “Blur area padding” to 20–30%. Large images are automatically tiled to catch small faces; profiles and heavy occlusion may still be missed.

Q: What is the difference between Accurate and Fast mode?
A: Accurate uses SSD MobileNetV1 (~5.4 MB) and is clearly better on small and profile faces — recommended for everyday use. Fast uses TinyFaceDetector (193 KB): quick to load and light on memory, best for close-up frontal faces or low-end devices.

Q: Does it reduce image quality?
A: No downscaling. Output keeps the original dimensions, saved as high-quality JPEG (95%).

Q: Can I process multiple images at once?
A: The current version handles one image at a time. Batch mode is in development.
