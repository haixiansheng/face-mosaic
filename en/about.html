About

A free, open-source, privacy-first online tool

Why we built this

Blurring faces before publishing a video is a common need: hiding bystanders in street footage, protecting children's privacy in family clips, or censoring customer faces in surveillance recordings. The usual options are paid software, or uploading your video to an unknown server — the latter carries a real privacy risk.

So we built this: all processing happens inside your browser. Your video never leaves your device. No sign-up, no fees, no usage limits.

How it works

The tool has three parts:

  1. Face detection — uses face-api.js with the TinyFaceDetector model (~190 KB) to scan each frame for faces. Frames are downsampled to 320 px wide for detection, balancing speed and accuracy.
  2. Blurring — draws the face regions on a Canvas using pixelation (downsample-and-upscale), multi-pass blur, or a solid black box.
  3. Video export — records the Canvas via the browser's MediaRecorder API and keeps the original audio track, producing a downloadable video file.

No server-side computation is involved, so it even works offline.

Disclaimer

This tool is provided “as is”, without any guarantee of detection accuracy. Automatic detection may miss faces (profiles, occlusion, very small faces) or produce false positives. Always review the output manually before publishing.
