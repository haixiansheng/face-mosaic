About

A free, open-source, privacy-first online tool

Why we built this

Blurring faces before publishing a photo is a common need: hiding bystanders in street shots, protecting children's privacy in family photos, or censoring customer faces in screenshots. The usual options are paid software, or uploading your image to an unknown server — the latter carries a real privacy risk.

So we built this: all processing happens inside your browser. Your image never leaves your device. No sign-up, no fees, no usage limits.

How it works

The tool has three parts:

  1. Face detection — uses face-api.js with the TinyFaceDetector model (~190 KB) to scan for faces. Images are downsampled to a 640 px longest side for detection, balancing speed and accuracy.
  2. Blurring — draws the face regions on a Canvas using pixelation (downsample-and-upscale), multi-pass blur, or a solid black box, at the original resolution so no detail is lost.
  3. Image export — exports the Canvas via toBlob() as a high-quality JPEG (95%) at the original resolution.

No server-side computation is involved, so it even works offline.
